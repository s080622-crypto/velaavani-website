About us

VelaaVani means “turtle haven” in Dhivehi. We started with a simple idea: a Maldives stay should not cost a resort budget, and it should leave the reef healthier than we found it.

Our guesthouse sits on Dhiffushi, a friendly local island in Kaafu Atoll, where fishing and family life shape the day. Guests eat with us, learn a few words of Dhivehi and swim with the same turtles our team has watched for years.

Our team

Local hosts, boat captains and marine naturalists who know every turtle by its markings.

Our hospitality

Warm, unhurried and honest. We explain island customs up front so you feel at ease on land and in the water.

Location & how to get here

Dhiffushi is about 45 minutes by speedboat from Velana International Airport (MLE).

  1. Meet & greet
    Find our host at Counter B-12 in the arrivals hall.
  2. Walk to the airport jetty
    About 2 minutes from arrivals.
  3. Speedboat to Dhiffushi
    Around 45 minutes. Scheduled seats cost $25–$35 per person, or charter a private boat.
  4. Buggy to VelaaVani
    An electric buggy takes you from the harbour to the guesthouse.

Transfer instructions

Send us your flight number and arrival time at least 48 hours ahead and we will book your boat seat. Scheduled departures vary by day and weather, so we confirm exact times with you by WhatsApp.

Arriving after dark? Ask for a private charter.

Policies

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 14:00. Check-out by 12:00. Early arrival or late departure can often be arranged: just ask.

Children

Children of all ages are welcome. Under-6s stay free in existing bedding. Kids must be supervised at the jetty and on snorkel trips.

Payment

A 30% deposit secures your booking; the balance is due at check-in in USD cash or by card. A 10% service charge and the Maldives tourist taxes apply.

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. Within 14 days, the first night is charged. No-shows are charged in full.

Island etiquette and alcohol

Bikinis are fine at the designated bikini beach and on boat trips; please dress modestly in the village. Alcohol is not sold on local islands. Floating bar yachts nearby serve guests.
